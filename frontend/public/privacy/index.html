1. Data Controller
2. General
Based on Article 13 of the Swiss Federal Constitution and the data protection provisions of the Swiss Confederation (Data Protection Act, DSG), every person is entitled to the protection of their privacy and to protection against misuse of their personal data. We comply with these provisions. Personal data is treated in strict confidence and is neither sold nor shared with third parties.
3. Legal Basis
Data processing is based on the following legal grounds:
  • Swiss DSG: Processing for contract performance and based on legitimate interests.
  • EU GDPR (for users in the EEA): Art. 6(1)(b) (contract performance), Art. 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in operating and securing the service).
4. Data Collected
RENEX collects as little personal data as possible. Specifically:
  • Handle (username): Freely chosen, used for identification within the app.
  • Passkey data: WebAuthn credentials are stored locally on your device. We only store the public key and a credential ID for authentication.
  • Messages: All messages are end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM + X25519 ECDH). The server stores only encrypted data that cannot be read without the participants' private keys.
  • Metadata: To deliver messages, communication relationships (who communicates with whom) and timestamps are stored. This metadata is technically necessary for the operation of the service.
  • Voice/video calls (WebRTC): Calls are established peer-to-peer. The server stores neither audio nor video content. For NAT traversal, the app contacts self-hosted STUN/TURN servers at turn.renex.id, operated at Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. With a direct connection, the server only briefly sees IP addresses for NAT discovery; in the relay fallback case (strict NAT), DTLS/SRTP-encrypted audio packets are forwarded, with plaintext keys negotiated exclusively between the two endpoints and inaccessible to the server. Call metadata (caller, recipient, time, duration) is stored locally on your device.
  • Recovery bundle (encrypted backup): Optionally, an encrypted backup of your conversation keys can be stored with the provider (Cloudflare R2) so you can restore your chat history via recovery phrase after device loss. The phrase never leaves your device; the backup is client-side encrypted and unreadable for the provider.
  • Push notifications (optional): If you enable notifications, a push subscription from your browser (endpoint + public key) is stored. Push payloads contain no message content, only a hint that a new message has arrived.
  • Technical data: IP address and user agent are temporarily processed in session data for the operation and security of the service. RENEX only logs technical metadata for stability and security, never message content or keys.
5. Purpose of Data Processing
The collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:
  • Providing and operating the messaging service
  • Authentication via WebAuthn/Passkeys
  • Delivering encrypted messages
  • Ensuring technical functionality and security
6. Encryption & Recovery
RENEX uses end-to-end encryption for all messages. Encryption keys are generated and stored exclusively on users' devices. The operator has no access to the plaintext of messages or to private keys at any time.
Recovery phrase: During initial setup, a 12-word phrase is generated on your device and shown once. A master key is derived from this phrase (PBKDF2 + HKDF) and used to encrypt a bundle of your conversation keys, which is stored with the provider (Cloudflare R2). The phrase never leaves your device and cannot be reconstructed by the provider. If you lose the phrase, recovering historical encrypted messages is technically impossible.
7. Data Retention and Deletion
  • Messages: Messages with auto-delete enabled are deleted client-side after the chosen period expires. Messages without auto-delete remain stored until the user deletes them or removes their account.
  • Account deletion: You can delete your account at any time within the app. All messages and contact data are removed. Your handle is reserved for 300 days to prevent identity abuse, then released.
  • Metadata: Communication relationships are cleaned up upon account deletion.
  • Technical data: IP addresses and user agent data in sessions are not stored permanently and expire with the session.
8. Cookies and Local Storage
RENEX does not use tracking cookies. The following technically necessary storage takes place:
  • Guest session cookie (guest_session): HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Strict, lifetime max. 24 hours. Set when you use an invite link as a guest; without this cookie no guest authentication is possible.
  • localStorage / sessionStorage / IndexedDB: encryption keys (locally encrypted with a device-bound storage key), handle, language preference, conversation keys. Never leave your device.

Hetzner processes IP addresses and connection metadata as a data processor under Art. 28 GDPR; the standard DPA addendum of the Hetzner terms is the contractual basis. Push payloads contain no message content. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can disable notifications at any time in your browser settings or in the app.
10. Data Security
RENEX implements the following technical and organizational measures to protect your data:
  • All connections are exclusively encrypted via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Message content is end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM + X25519 ECDH) — the server cannot decrypt it.
  • Private keys never leave the user's device.
  • Authentication is exclusively via WebAuthn/Passkeys — no passwords are stored.
  • Infrastructure is operated through Cloudflare, with DDoS protection and Web Application Firewall.
RENEX does not store connection logs. Neither IP addresses nor access logs are permanently recorded. Technical data in sessions expires automatically with the session.
11. Data Sharing
Personal data is not sold, traded, or otherwise transferred to third parties, unless there is a legal obligation to do so.
12. International Data Transfer
Data may be transferred to the USA in connection with the third-party services mentioned in section 9. This transfer is secured by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and/or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, the UK Extension, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which the third-party providers used are certified. For users in Switzerland, the Swiss Data Protection Act (DSG) additionally applies; the Swiss-U.S. DPF ensures that data transfers to the USA meet the requirements of the DSG.
